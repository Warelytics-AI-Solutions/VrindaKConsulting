How We Work

A Clear Process. Practical Outcomes.

Good consulting should lead to action. Every engagement follows a structured process.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We begin by understanding your business, goals, challenges and current position.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We review the relevant business functions, identify gaps and understand the issues that need attention.

  3. 03

    Plan

    We develop a practical strategy, define priorities and agree on the scope of work.

  4. 04

    Execute

    We support implementation directly or coordinate with qualified specialists and delivery partners, based on the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Review & Improve

    We review progress, track agreed outcomes and identify the next steps.

Some challenges need an accountant, an advocate or a developer. You should not have to manage every professional independently.

Vrinda K Consulting leads the relationship and coordinates the right specialists. Work is delivered directly, jointly or by an independent qualified professional.

For regulated professional work, the relevant specialist is responsible for their professional advice, review, certification, filing or representation. The scope, fees and responsibilities are clarified before work begins.
